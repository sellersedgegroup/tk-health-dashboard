Reserve inventory — by SKU

26 SKUs

Amazon inventory pacing (SP-API)

Real Amazon on-hand + inbound — account-wide
How the SKU table below is calculated: target cover is 90 days — the buffer we always want on hand. Reorder point is target cover plus an assumed 60-day supplier lead time (production + shipping to the reserve warehouse) = 150 days of cover — order from the supplier once a SKU's reserve drops to that level, so stock lands before you dip under the 90-day floor. Overstock is flagged above 270 days (nine months) of cover, where capital and expiry risk start to outweigh the safety margin.

⚠ Data gap: stock counts below are from The Fulfillment Lab (3PL reserve warehouse, Tampa) as of 6 August 2026 — this is what's available to ship into Amazon, not what's already sitting in Amazon's FBA network. The panel above now pulls real Amazon on-hand + inbound units via SP-API — but Data Brill Core's per-product grouping isn't configured for this account, so that feed currently returns one blended account-wide total rather than a true per-SKU breakdown (it does flag the single worst-runway ASIN within the blend). Until that grouping is fixed, the per-SKU table below still uses 3PL reserve stock as the best available per-SKU proxy — read its "reserve cover" as time until the 3PL runs out, not total time until an Amazon stockout (FBA holds additional unseen units on top of this).

Lead time is a placeholder assumption — tell your assistant the account's real supplier lead time and this recalculates.

How this is calculated: Gross profit = Total sales − COGS (units sold × landed cost/unit) − Amazon referral fee (assumed flat 15% of sales, Health & Household standard) − ad spend. Margin % = gross profit ÷ total sales.

⚠ Data gap: COGS is from the cost sheet you uploaded (Sellerboard export, per-SKU landed cost — most recent effective cost period used per SKU). This margin figure excludes FBA fulfillment (pick-pack) and monthly storage fees, since no per-SKU weight/dimension data is connected yet — so it's landed-cost margin after referral fee and ad spend, not full net profit after every Amazon fee. Share FBA fee-per-unit or weight/size tier per SKU to tighten this further.
